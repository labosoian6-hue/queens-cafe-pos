1. Introduction & System Overview

The Queens Cafe Hotel POS system is a web-based point of sale system. It runs on a computer and is accessed through a browser (Chrome or Edge) at:

http://localhost:5000

Who Uses What

RoleWhat They DoAccess
WaiterTakes orders, sends to kitchen, prints order slipPOS Screen
CashierProcesses payments, prints receiptsPOS + Payment Screen
Kitchen StaffViews orders on kitchen displayKitchen Screen
Admin/ManagerFull access — menu, staff, reports, settingsAll Screens

Default Login

UsernamePasswordRole
adminadmin123Admin / Manager
Important: Change the default password immediately after first login. Go to Settings → Change Password.

2. How to Log In

1
Open the browser (Chrome or Edge) on the cafe computer.
2
Type http://localhost:5000 in the address bar and press Enter.
3
The login page will appear. Enter your Username and Password.
4
Click Login. You will be taken to the Dashboard.
Note: The system will lock automatically after 15 minutes of no activity. Just log in again.

How to Log Out

Click your name at the bottom of the left menu → click Logout.

3. Taking an Order (Waiter)

1
From the left menu click Take Order (POS).
2
A list of tables will appear. Click the table number where the customer is sitting. For takeaway click Takeaway.
3
The order screen opens. On the left side you will see the menu items.
4
Use the category buttons at the top to filter by food type (Chicken, Beef, Fish, Drinks, etc.).
5
Tap/click an item to add it to the order. It will appear on the right side (Order Ticket).
6
To increase quantity tap the + button next to the item. To reduce tap −. To remove tap ×.
7
To add a special instruction (e.g. no onions), tap the 📝 note icon next to the item and type the instruction.
Tip: Use the search bar at the top to quickly find any item by name.

Searching for Items

Type part of the item name in the search bar. For example type "fish" to see all fish items.

4. Sending Order to Kitchen

Once all items are added to the order:

1
Click the 🍳 Send button (orange button) at the bottom of the Order Ticket.
2
Two print windows will open automatically:
  • Kitchen Ticket — prints for kitchen staff (shows items + quantities)
  • Customer Order Slip — prints for the customer (shows items + total to pay later)
3
Make sure the printer is on. The tickets will print automatically.
4
Give the Customer Order Slip to the customer. Take the Kitchen Ticket to the kitchen (or the kitchen screen will show it).
Note: The customer pays AFTER dining, not at the time of ordering.

5. Processing Payment (Cashier)

When the customer is done dining and ready to pay:

1
Go to Take Order (POS) from the left menu.
2
Click on the customer's table. The order will open.
3
Click the green PAY button at the bottom right.
4
Select the payment method:
  • Cash — enter amount given by customer, system calculates change
  • M-Pesa — enter M-Pesa reference number
  • Card — enter card reference
5
Click Confirm Payment. The receipt will print automatically.
6
Give the receipt to the customer.

Giving a Discount

On the order screen, enter a discount amount before clicking PAY.

  • Select % for percentage discount or KSh for fixed amount
  • Discounts above 10% require a Manager PIN

Split Bill

If customers want to split the bill, click the ⚡ Split button and follow the instructions on screen.

6. Kitchen Display (Kitchen Staff)

1
From the left menu click Kitchen Display.
2
All open orders are shown as cards. Each card shows the table number, items, and time.
3
When an order is ready, click Order Ready (bump button) on that card.
4
The order is removed from the kitchen screen and the waiter is notified.
Note: The kitchen screen refreshes automatically every 30 seconds. You can also refresh manually.

7. Menu Management (Admin Only)

Adding a New Item

1
From the left menu click Menu & Prices.
2
Click Add Item.
3
Enter the item name, price, and category.
4
Click Save.

Editing a Price

1
Go to Menu & Prices.
2
Find the item and click Edit.
3
Change the price and click Save.

Marking Item as Unavailable

If an item is out of stock, click Edit next to the item and uncheck Available. It will be hidden from the order screen until you make it available again.

8. Staff Management (Admin Only)

Adding a New Staff Member

1
From the left menu click Staff.
2
Click Add Staff.
3
Enter full name, username, password, and role (Admin or Waiter).
4
Click Save.

Staff Roles

RoleCan Do
AdminEverything — menu, staff, reports, settings, voids
WaiterTake orders, send to kitchen, process payments

9. Reports & Z-Report (Admin Only)

Daily Sales Report

1
Click Reports from the left menu.
2
Select the date range and click Generate.
3
You can see total sales, number of orders, and top-selling items.
4
Click Export CSV to download the report to Excel.

Z-Report (End of Day)

The Z-Report is run at the end of every working day to close the shift.

1
Click Z-Report from the left menu.
2
Click Generate Z-Report.
3
Print the report and file it for records.
Important: Run the Z-Report every day before closing. Do not skip this step.

10. Important Rules & Tips

Daily Opening Procedure

  1. Turn on the computer
  2. Open Chrome or Edge browser
  3. Go to http://localhost:5000
  4. Log in with your username and password
  5. You are ready to take orders

Daily Closing Procedure

  1. Make sure all orders are paid
  2. Run the Z-Report (Admin)
  3. Print and file the Z-Report
  4. Log out of the system
  5. Close the browser

Common Issues & Solutions

ProblemSolution
System not opening in browserMake sure the black command window is still open. If closed, double-click the Queens Cafe POS shortcut on Desktop.
Forgot passwordCall the manager/admin to reset it from the Staff page.
Item not showing on POSAdmin must check if the item is marked as Available in Menu & Prices.
Printer not printingCheck printer is on and connected. Try clicking Print again.
Session locked / logged outNormal after 15 minutes. Just log in again.
Wrong item added to orderTap × next to the item to remove it before sending to kitchen.

Rules for All Staff

  • Never share your password with anyone
  • Always log out when leaving the counter
  • Never void an order without manager approval
  • Always give the customer their receipt
  • Report any system problems to the manager immediately
Remember: If you are unsure about anything, ask the manager before proceeding. It is better to ask than to make a mistake.
